Noctalia's dragSource and dropZone (payload, accepts, onDrop) make reordering cards possible in the panel, but the material shader applies its stages in a fixed order that its render-pipeline doc argues cannot change for blur and roughness. Only worth doing when niri-material gains reorderable stages, and then the bus needs an order value.
